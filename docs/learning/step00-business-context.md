# Step 0: Business Context

## The Problem
A bank's customer service receives 4.2 lakh (420,000) calls per month.
65% of these are 3 repetitive questions:
1. "What's my balance?"
2. "What was this debit from my account?"
3. "Send me a cheque book."

## Why Customers Still Call
The net-banking app has 14 screens. Customers find it easier to call than navigate.

## The Solution
Build a secure, conversational AI chatbot that answers banking queries naturally.

## Learning Goals
- Understand the business problem driving this project
- Identify the gap between existing tools and customer expectations
- Define success metrics for the AI solution
